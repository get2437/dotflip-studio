## Pixel-Art Converter processing engine

Source: https://github.com/AleksTomatoOnion/Pixel_Art_Converter

Pinned revision: c6b074e3348b59d1b45a696aa08f587e3035ead1.

DOTFLIP vendors the pure processing modules (Kuwahara flattening, cel-shading bands, contour extraction, despeckling, downsampling and outlines), adapted for transparent sprite sequences and a shared palette. The upstream logo and UI artwork are not included. Source hashes and modifications are recorded in app/vendor/pixel-art-converter/UPSTREAM.json; the full license is also shipped beside the runtime modules.
